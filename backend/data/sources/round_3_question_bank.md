# VYUHAM '26 | PROMPT WAR

## Round 3 Question Bank (FINAL): Live Word Ban Showdown

**Time Limit:** 2 Minutes per Challenge
**Format:** Live On-Stage Finale

---

### Operational Overview & Rules

* **Objective:** Complete a surprise AI task without using specified banned words.
* **Qualification:** Top 3–5 teams from Rounds 1 and 2 qualify for the finale.
* **Execution:** Finalists compete on stage. All receive the same task and banned-word list simultaneously.
* **Submission:** Exactly 2 minutes to construct and submit 1 final prompt.
* **Key Constraints:**
  * Outputs containing banned words receive **ZERO** marks for compliance.
  * Banned words are checked regardless of capitalization or pluralization.
  * Manual editing of the output is strictly prohibited.
* **Evaluation (100 Marks):** Task Accuracy (30) | Banned-Word Compliance (30) | Prompt Effectiveness (20) | Creativity & Engagement (20).

---

### Challenge 1: The Nameless Ocean

* **AI Task:** Summarize the plot of the movie *Titanic* in exactly three sentences.
* **Banned Words:** `Ship`, `Boat`, `Sink`, `Ice`, `Water`, `Ocean`, `Rose`, `Jack`, `Door`, `Drown`, `Sea`, `Film`, `Movie`
* **Prompt Engineering Challenge:** Describe the maritime disaster story without using maritime terms, character names, or film industry terms. Force the AI to use terms like "luxurious vessel," "frozen obstacle," "young lovers," and "tragic outcome."

---

### Challenge 2: The Forbidden Loop

* **AI Task:** Explain the programming concept of "Recursion" to a beginner.
* **Banned Words:** `Recursion`, `Recursive`, `Function`, `Call`, `Itself`, `Loop`, `Repeat`, `Base`, `Case`, `Code`, `Program`, `Software`
* **Prompt Engineering Challenge:** Explaining recursion without standard programming jargon is extremely difficult. Guide the AI toward physical analogies (such as Russian nesting dolls or parallel mirrors reflecting infinitely).

---

### Challenge 3: The Censor’s Recipe

* **AI Task:** Write a step-by-step guide on how to prepare a cup of tea.
* **Banned Words:** `Tea`, `Water`, `Boil`, `Cup`, `Mug`, `Hot`, `Drink`, `Pour`, `Leaf`, `Leaves`, `Heat`, `Warm`, `Liquid`, `Beverage`
* **Prompt Engineering Challenge:** Describe an everyday brewing process using abstract terminology ("herbal infusion," "thermal energy," "ceramic vessel") without triggering the AI's standard recipe templates.

---

### Challenge 4: The Moneyless Market

* **AI Task:** Explain how the economic concept of "Inflation" works.
* **Banned Words:** `Money`, `Cash`, `Coin`, `Dollar`, `Rupee`, `Price`, `Cost`, `Expensive`, `Buy`, `Sell`, `Inflation`, `Value`, `Currency`
* **Prompt Engineering Challenge:** The AI's training data for inflation heavily relies on monetary terms. Force it to explain purchasing power devaluation using bartering principles (e.g., trading grain for livestock).

### Challenge 5: The Invisible Web

* **Banned Words:** `Internet`, `Web`, `Wi-Fi`, `Computer`, `Phone`, `Online`, `Connect`, `Network`, `Router`, `Screen`, `Signal`, `Digital`
* **Prompt Engineering Challenge:** Force the AI into a fairytale or fantasy analogy (such as invisible enchanted messengers delivering notes between distant kingdom towers) to completely avoid technology jargon.

### Challenge 6: The Bloodless Monster

### Challenge 7: The Colorless Rainbow

### Challenge 8: The Oxygen-Free Breath
